# Voila Floor — Hyper-local SEO & Backlink Strategy (2026)

## Objective
Own useful local search visibility for **specialist floor cleaning, restoration and maintenance in Geraldton and the Mid West** without doorway pages, bought links or fabricated local relevance.

## Search positioning
Primary commercial themes:
- carpet cleaning Geraldton;
- wool carpet cleaning Geraldton;
- carpet stain / urine treatment Geraldton;
- tile and grout cleaning Geraldton;
- natural stone cleaning / sealing Geraldton;
- vinyl strip and seal Geraldton;
- concrete / epoxy floor cleaning Geraldton;
- gym / rubber floor cleaning Geraldton;
- upholstery / leather cleaning Geraldton;
- commercial / strata floor cleaning Geraldton;
- commercial floor maintenance Geraldton.

The site architecture uses one strong Geraldton service-area hub plus genuinely differentiated service pages. **Do not create near-identical suburb doorway pages.** Create a location page only when there is enough real local evidence, projects, FAQs, commercial context or service difference to make it useful on its own.

## Google Business Profile — P0
1. Establish/verify the independent Voila Floor profile when the trading position allows it.
2. Use the exact trading name, correct primary category and only relevant secondary categories.
3. Keep service area, hours, contact details and website consistent with the website and other citations.
4. Add real project photographs regularly; do not use AI-generated before/after evidence.
5. Add the core service catalogue.
6. Ask eligible customers for reviews through the normal post-job workflow; never gate reviews or offer incentives for positive ratings.
7. Respond to reviews with useful, individual replies.
8. Add UTM parameters to the website link so GBP traffic can be measured.

Google references:
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business

## Local authority / citation targets
Prioritise relevance and legitimacy over volume.

### Tier 1 — Geraldton / Mid West relationships
- **Mid West Chamber of Commerce and Industry (MWCCI):** membership/profile/directory listing if commercially justified.
- **LocalWA:** accurate Geraldton business listing if the directory accepts the business and data can be kept current.
- **City/community organisations:** sponsorship or supplier references only where there is a real relationship or project.
- **Local property managers, strata managers, accommodation operators, gyms, flooring retailers and facilities contractors:** useful partner/resource pages or case-study links where mutually relevant.
- **Local media:** pitch genuinely useful local stories or data, not generic press-release link placement.

### Tier 2 — Industry relevance
- **Strata Community Association WA:** supplier presence/membership only if Voila Floor genuinely services the strata market and the commercial case stacks up.
- Manufacturer/distributor partner directories for equipment, chemistry, flooring/sealing systems where Voila Floor is genuinely approved/eligible.
- Flooring, cleaning, facility-management and property-industry associations where membership has business value beyond the backlink.

## Backlink acquisition plays
### 1. Evidence-led case studies
Publish a strong case study, obtain the client's permission, then offer the client/contractor a concise project reference they can link to. Never invent a client name, result or endorsement.

### 2. Geraldton facility guides
Examples:
- commercial carpet maintenance planning for Geraldton offices;
- floor-care planning for strata common areas;
- maintaining rubber gym flooring in coastal WA conditions;
- tile/grout maintenance for accommodation and hospitality sites.

These can be useful resources for property managers, facilities contractors and local organisations to reference.

### 3. Technical partner content
Create co-authored or supplier-reviewed articles only where there is a genuine technical relationship. Link to primary manufacturer guidance and ask the partner to reference the useful resource if appropriate.

### 4. Local sponsorship/community links
Support a relevant Geraldton event, club, community organisation or facility only when the sponsorship makes sense commercially/community-wise. A link is a secondary benefit, not the reason for the transaction.

### 5. Contractor ecosystem
Develop relationships with general cleaners, flooring retailers/installers, builders, restoration contractors, real-estate/strata teams and facility managers. Create a clear specialist subcontract capability page they can reference.

## Prohibited / low-value tactics
Do not:
- buy followed links or bulk directory packages;
- run excessive reciprocal link exchanges;
- use automated link-building software;
- publish keyword-swapped guest posts at scale;
- distribute press releases mainly for optimised anchor-text links;
- create fake local offices or suburb pages;
- fabricate reviews, projects, accreditations or awards;
- generate hundreds of low-information AI blog posts.

If a paid placement is legitimate advertising/sponsorship, the link relationship must be appropriately qualified.

## Internal linking rules
Every article should link naturally to:
- the relevant service page;
- the Geraldton service-area page where appropriate;
- a related case study once available;
- the enquiry page where the reader has decision intent.

Every case study should link back to its service family and any article that explains the method.

## Case-study publishing standard
Required before publishing:
- verified service and substrate;
- date/general location that does not expose private information;
- starting condition;
- chosen method and why;
- relevant limitations / exclusions;
- before and after evidence where available;
- truthful outcome wording;
- client permission for identifiable details/images;
- no unsupported percentage improvement or performance claim.

## 90-day launch SEO sequence
### Days 0–14
- final domain and contact details;
- production website;
- Google Search Console verification;
- submit sitemap;
- establish/transition independent Google Business Profile when permitted;
- confirm LocalBusiness schema / canonical URLs / indexing;
- create accurate core citations.

### Days 15–45
- publish first 3–5 useful local articles;
- capture first verified Voila Floor case-study evidence;
- approach MWCCI / legitimate local directories;
- establish first contractor/property relationships;
- collect independent-business reviews through compliant workflow.

### Days 46–90
- publish verified case study(s);
- outreach to relevant partner/supplier/client pages;
- identify Search Console queries with impressions but weak CTR/rank;
- improve the service pages around those real queries;
- test one genuinely useful Geraldton industry/resource piece worthy of citations.

## Measurement
Track monthly:
- organic website enquiries;
- Google Business Profile calls/clicks/directions where available;
- branded vs non-branded query impressions/clicks;
- service-page conversion rate;
- referring domains and the business relevance of each;
- review count, rating and response rate;
- case-study assisted conversions;
- lead-to-quote and quote-to-job rate by source;
- revenue by acquisition source.

SEO success is **qualified work and stronger local brand equity**, not page count or backlink count.
